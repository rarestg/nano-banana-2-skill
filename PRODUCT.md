# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Developers generating images and icons for websites. They use the workbench to create alternatives, compare them side by side, and choose one production-ready asset without a cumbersome review process.

## Product Purpose

Nano Banana 2 helps developers move quickly from an image brief to a selected, production-ready website asset. Success means finding one suitable image quickly, with comparison and export supporting that decision rather than slowing it down.

## Positioning

Structured side-by-side image comparison and a fast path from generation to production export in one local workbench.

## Brand Personality

Focused, simple, parallel. The interface should stay out of the way while making simultaneous generation and comparison easy to understand.

## Anti-references

Avoid clunky workflows, hard-to-use controls, dated presentation, and unnecessary complexity.

## Design Principles

- Optimize the shortest path from brief to selected asset.
- Make parallel alternatives easy to compare fairly.
- Keep controls simple while preserving essential generation context.
- Make the chosen asset and its export path unmistakable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA as the baseline. Support complete keyboard operation, visible focus states, reduced motion, and status communication that does not rely on color alone.
